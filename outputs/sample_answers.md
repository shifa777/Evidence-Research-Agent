# Sample Cited Answers

These are reproducible demonstration questions for the included corpus. The live application generates the answer from retrieved passages and attaches source/page citations from the actual PDFs.

## 1. What are the major risks and challenges of generative AI in education?

The provided UNESCO guidance identifies challenges including privacy protection, unequal access, bias and discrimination, unclear regulation, questions around transparency, and effects on assessment and learning. It recommends a human-centred approach, institutional validation, data protection, and development of AI competencies.

**Evidence:** UNESCO, *Guidance for generative AI in education and research*, especially pp. 2, 14–27 and 36–37.

## 2. What AI competencies should students develop?

UNESCO's student framework organizes competencies around a human-centred mindset, ethics of AI, AI techniques and applications, and AI system design. It also defines progression through Understand, Apply and Create levels.

**Evidence:** UNESCO, *AI competency framework for students*, pp. 18–25.

## 3. What trustworthy AI characteristics are emphasized by NIST?

NIST's AI Risk Management Framework identifies characteristics including validity and reliability, safety, security and resilience, accountability and transparency, explainability and interpretability, privacy enhancement, and fairness with harmful bias managed.

**Evidence:** NIST, *AI Risk Management Framework 1.0*, pp. 12–18.

## 4. What does the U.S. Department of Education discuss about AI and teaching and learning?

The report examines how AI can support teaching and learning while emphasizing human judgement, responsible implementation, evaluation, and attention to risks and limitations. It is intended to inform educators, leaders, policymakers, researchers, and education technology developers.

**Evidence:** U.S. Department of Education, *Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Teaching and Learning*, 2023.
